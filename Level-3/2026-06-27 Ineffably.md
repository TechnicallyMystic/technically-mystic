# [Ineffably](https://technicallymystic.com/p/ineffably) 

Subtitle: [Level 3: Union] ...

Posted: June 27, 2026

---

When you are experiencing without words, without thoughts, without language, with just pure *being*, you are experiencing God.

God is the only One Who can be communicated with in this way.
God is the only One Who *is* communicated with in this way.

Pure, radiant, intense *being*.

My God... all words fail to serve You. Let me serve You with my being.
