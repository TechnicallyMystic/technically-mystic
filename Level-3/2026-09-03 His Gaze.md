# [His Gaze](https://technicallymystic.com/p/his-gaze)

Subtitle: [Level 3: Union]

Posted: September 3, 2026

***

The concept of God
Does not make me tremble
In awe or fear;
Eye contact does.

So I decided
To make eye contact
With Him.

Unbroken.
Yet it was I who broke.

His Gaze became mine
I saw my life
Lived, loved
Though the Eyes of Eternity.

Prolonged.
Unbroken.
